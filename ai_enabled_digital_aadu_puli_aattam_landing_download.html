Rulebook & Strategy

The Asymmetric Battle: Puli vs Aadu

Unlike symmetric games such as Chess or Checkers, Aadu Puli Aattam pits unequal forces against one another. Strength lies in unity for the goats, while speed and isolation govern the tigers.

🐅

The Tigers (3 Pieces) — Capture by Leaping

Tigers move along adjacent connecting lines. If an adjacent position holds a solitary goat and the linear position directly beyond is unoccupied, the tiger jumps and permanently captures that goat. Tigers win upon capturing 5 goats!

🐐

The Goats (15 Pieces) — Encircle & Paralyze

Phase 1 involves placing all 15 goats onto vacant nodes one by one. Once placed, Phase 2 permits tactical movement to adjacent spots. Goats cannot capture; their goal is to surround all 3 tigers until zero legal moves remain.

How The Minimax Opponent Thinks

A look under the hood of the decision-making pipeline documented in Chapter 4 of the research report.

Step 01

Game Tree Exploration

From the current board state, the engine systematically generates all legal successor moves for tigers and goats down to depth d.

List<Move> moves = GenerateLegalMoves(state);
Step 02

Alpha-Beta Branch Pruning

Tracks the best guaranteed values (α and β). If β ≤ α, further subtrees cannot alter the optimal choice and are immediately culled.

if (beta <= alpha) break; // Pruned branch
Step 03

Evaluation Heuristic

Weighs goat containment metrics, tiger maneuverability corridors, and vulnerable goat clusters to pick the highest outcome move in milliseconds.
